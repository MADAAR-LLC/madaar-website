About Madaar

Madaar LLC is a Texas technology company building an online marketplace for commercial trucking roadside assistance. Truck owners request help inside the app, and nearby independent service providers accept the job and come to the truck — wherever it stopped.

Services

Towing & Recovery

Professional towing and recovery for commercial trucks, when the truck cannot move under its own power.

Mobile Mechanic

Qualified mechanics who come to the truck and fix it on the spot, wherever it is parked.

Tire Service

On-site tire repair and replacement for commercial trucks, day and night.

Parts Sourcing & Delivery

Needed parts sourced from nearby parts stores and delivered to the truck's location.

How It Works

  1. Request in the app. The truck owner describes the problem and the truck's location inside the Madaar app.
  2. A nearby provider accepts. An independent provider close by accepts the job and heads to the truck.
  3. Back on the road. The provider completes the work and the driver gets moving again.
